# PAO notes

One line per number, shown in the codex under the three role rows. Every note
is a specific, checkable hook — a documented number, date, count, title, record,
or an explicit letter/character count. Numbers without one are omitted rather
than filled with a guess.

Letter/character-count notes are labeled as such. They are wordplay, not
history. Where a book is a third-party tribute, the note says so.
