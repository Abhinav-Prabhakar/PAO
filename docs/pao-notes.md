# PAO notes

One line per role, shown in the codex under the three ASCII rows. A number can
carry up to three notes — one for its Person, one for its Action, one for its
Object. Only specific, checkable hooks live here; a role without one is omitted
rather than filled with a guess.

Letter/character-count notes are wordplay and are labeled as such. The full
research record — every kept row, every rejected candidate, every searched
no-hook, and the 100-row coverage ledger — is in
[`pao-facts-v3.md`](./pao-facts-v3.md).

## Notes on specific rows

- **01** uses the SI unit: one tesla is the unit of magnetic flux density.
- **08 A** uses the American painters known as The Eight, whose exhibition
  toured in 1908. The earlier source pointed at the unrelated Hungarian group of
  the same name and was corrected.
- **45 O** uses Galileo's first telescopic observation of Messier 45, the
  Pleiades.
- **75** is explicit digit wordplay: Indiana has 7 letters, Jones has 5, giving
  7 then 5. It is not addition.
- **95** is kept at the user’s request. The book is a third-party tribute
  collection titled *95 Inspired Creations*, not a book written by Ramsay.
- **99** has three separate hooks: Socrates was executed in 399 BCE; Challenger
  (OV-099) broke apart 73 seconds after launch; and Challenger was NASA orbiter
  OV-099.
